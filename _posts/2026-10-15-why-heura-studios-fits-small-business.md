If you run a small business, you already know the problem. The same questions arrive every day: *What's your return policy? Do you ship to my area? How do I reset my password?* The answers are written down somewhere. They just take a person's time to find and send.

An AI assistant can take a lot of that off your plate. But most of the options ask you to hand your documents, your customer conversations and your pricing to someone else's servers, pay a monthly fee that grows with your usage, and hope it keeps working the way you set it up.

We built Heura Studios for a different kind of business owner: one who wants the help without giving up control.

And it isn't only for customer questions. The behind-the-scenes work that usually waits for an engineer, or for whoever is best with spreadsheets, is often just rules applied to data, and an assistant that knows your rules can handle much of it.

## 1. It can run on your own computer

Our base model, Heura Arche, is small enough to run on a laptop\*. When you run it that way, your documents and conversations stay on your device and are not sent to us (apart from licence checks and crash reports you choose to send). For a clinic, a law or accounting practice, a shop with customer details, or anyone who simply prefers their business information stay theirs, that is a real difference.

## 2. It learns *your* business, not the internet's

You don't need to write code or prompts from scratch. You teach your assistant with what you already have: your documents, your rules ("never promise a delivery date"), and a few examples of good answers. Starter kits for support, HR, IT and sales give you a head start so you aren't staring at a blank page.

## 3. It can take on the backend busywork

A lot of small-business work is really back-office logic: rules applied to data. In many shops it falls to a part-time developer, a contractor, or whoever is best with spreadsheets. Heura can take on routine jobs like these:

- **Checking invoices:** work out the subtotal, discount and tax, and flag when a supplier's total doesn't match.
- **Reorder decisions:** combine stock on hand, stock already promised to customers, and supplier lead time to say what to order.
- **Ticket routing:** sort incoming requests by your own priority rules and send each to the right person.
- **Customer list cleanup:** spot duplicates and missing emails before a mailing goes out.
- **Reports without the risk:** summarize issues as counts, without exposing customer names or emails.
- **Deadlines:** work out when a reply is due, counting only your business hours.
- **Approval checks:** confirm a refund has the right sign-off before anyone processes it.

**How we keep it dependable.** You write the rules once, in plain language. The assistant applies them and shows its work. Exact arithmetic goes to a calculator or script instead of being guessed by the model, anything that moves money or changes records waits for a person to approve it, and you can run the exam on these tasks just like customer questions.

**What it isn't.** It won't design your database, build integrations or secure your systems; for that you still want a professional. Heura is for the routine layer in between: work that doesn't need an engineer every time, but does need to be right. If a task calls for a small script, a larger model you connect can draft it, and a person should review it before it runs.

## 4. You can check it before customers see it

The question we hear most is *"how do I know it won't say something wrong?"* Fair. Heura Studios includes an exam: you test the assistant on realistic questions, see how it does, and fix what it gets wrong before it goes live on your website. We think you should be able to measure an assistant, not just hope.

## 5. You're not locked in

Heura works with the model that fits the job. Run Arche on your own machine, or bring Claude, GPT, Gemini or a model on your own server. Your teaching, your tests and the chat bubble on your website work the same way either way. If a bigger model suits a task better, you can switch without starting over.

## 6. Your data is not our product

Using your data to improve our models is off unless you turn it on. Passwords, credentials and payment details are never used for training. Your custom assistants are not shared with other customers.

## 7. Simple, predictable cost

Because Arche runs on your own computer, there is no per-message bill for the model itself. If you want a stronger model, you can still use your API keys for Claude or ChatGPT and we will cover your tokens for up to 5 hours of thinking.

## An honest note on who it's for

A small model on a laptop is best at focused, repeatable work where the answer lives in your own documents: FAQs, internal policies, sorting messages, first drafts that a person reviews. It isn't the right tool for complex reasoning, exact calculations or anything where a wrong answer is costly and nobody checks it. For those, use the exam, keep a person in the loop, or choose a larger model inside Heura. We'd rather tell you that now than have you find out later.

## What to expect from the first month

1. **Pick one job**, such as answering questions about your policies.
2. **Add your documents and rules** and choose a starter kit.
3. **Run the exam** and fix the weak spots.
4. **Switch it on** for your website chat, with a person checking the answers at first.

\*Runs on modest hardware, for example an integrated GPU with 12 execution units (EUs), a 200 MHz base clock boosting to 650–700 MHz, shared system RAM (no dedicated VRAM) and a 14 nm process. Training may require better specs, but you can do it on something like a MacBook Pro.
