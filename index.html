How it works

Everything happens in Heura Studios, from your first document to a chat on your website. No setup, no code.

  1. 1

    Pick

    Name your AI and pick where it starts: a blank slate, or a kit for support, HR, IT or sales.

  2. 2

    Teach

    Drop in your documents, write rules in plain English and add example answers. It uses each one in its very next answer.

  3. 3

    Train

    Give it an exam to see how ready it is, fix what it gets wrong, then train your lessons right into it.

  4. 4

    Launch

    Put a chat bubble on your website, connect your app through an API, or run it on your own computer.

Models

Under the hood, your AI runs on Heura Arche, a model built on the open Qwen3-VL-2B and fine-tuned for business answers. Use it in Heura Studios or download it to your own computer. Two stronger ones are on the way.

Or bring your own

Any AI can answer with another model instead, using your company’s own key. Teaching, tests and the chat on your website work the same.

  • Claude

    Anthropic’s models, with your Anthropic key.

  • GPT

    OpenAI’s models, with your OpenAI key. GPT can also be trained on your examples.

  • Gemini

    Google’s models, with a Google AI Studio key.

  • Your own model

    Any model you run with Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM or another OpenAI-compatible server.
