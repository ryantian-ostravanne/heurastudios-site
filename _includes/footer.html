{%- comment -%} The footer (always dark), drawn as a pipeline in the Studio: its sections are nodes on the canvas, wired in order (Heura Studios, then Product, Legal and Contact) and across (Heura Studios to Legal, dashed, and to Contact, on a long loop under the row; Product to Contact, over Legal), with wires running in from the screen's left edge and out past its right edge on wide screens. A wire that passes a node runs straight under or over it (.ov-lane, at its height on the canvas, --lane); the nodes sit at fixed heights (site.css) and are fixed sizes, so the drawings meet. On a phone the nodes stack, one wire down between each. Then the copyright and the Appearance setting (System / Light / Dark, run by site.js). The wires are decoration (aria-hidden); each node is what it was: a heading and its links. Styles: site.css, "footer". {%- endcomment -%}
